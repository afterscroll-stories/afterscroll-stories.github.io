Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how AfterScroll Stories handles information when its owner uses private creator tools connected to YouTube.

2. Information Used

The creator tools use Google OAuth and YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel. Information processed may include authorized account access tokens, video files, titles, descriptions, hashtags and video visibility settings.

3. Purpose of Data Use

This information is used exclusively to prepare and publish video content to the owner's YouTube channel.

4. Storage and Security

The tools operate in a privately managed environment. Authorization credentials are stored in the local n8n credential storage. Video files and related content may be stored locally during processing.

5. Sharing of Information

Information required for publishing is transmitted to Google and YouTube through the YouTube Data API. Personal information is not sold or used for advertising. The tools are not offered as a public service.

6. Data Deletion and Access Revocation

Locally stored files and credentials can be deleted by the owner from the private environment. Google account access can be revoked here: Google Account Connections .

7. Third-Party Policies

Use of Google services is subject to Google's Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy .

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the channel owner through the official AfterScroll Stories channel.
