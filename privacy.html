Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how AfterScroll Stories handles information in connection with its YouTube channel and related publishing activities.

2. Information and Data Used

The channel owner uses Google OAuth and YouTube API Services to access the YouTube account and publish videos. Information processed for these purposes may include authorization tokens, video files, titles, descriptions, hashtags and video visibility settings.

3. Purpose of Processing

The information is used solely to manage the channel's video publishing activities, including uploading videos and setting their associated metadata.

4. Storage and Security

Authorization credentials are stored in a privately managed environment controlled by the channel owner. Video files and related content may also be stored locally during processing. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect this information.

5. Sharing of Information

Information necessary for publishing videos is transmitted to Google and YouTube through their official API services. Information is not sold or shared with advertising companies or data brokers.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Authorization credentials are retained while access to the YouTube account is required and can be removed by the account owner. Local video files and related records can be deleted from the private storage environment. Published videos remain subject to the channel owner's YouTube account settings and deletion choices.

Google account access can be revoked here: Google Account Connections .
